How the table is built

  • Mentions come from the ApeWisdom r/wallstreetbets tracker, which scans about twice an hour and counts comments as well as posts. A mention is attention. It is not a measure of buying or of sentiment.
  • Insider rows come from SEC Form 4 filings for each company, read straight from EDGAR, for the 90 days before the week ends. Buys are open-market purchases (code P). Sales are open-market sales (code S).
  • 10b5-1 plan sales are sales the filing marks as made under a pre-set trading plan. They are part of the sales total and shown on their own.
  • Set apart means sales made the same day as an option exercise, and shares sold to cover taxes. They are kept out of the label because they say little about what an insider thinks.
  • Labels describe the filings only. "Insiders bought" or "Insiders sold" when one side is at least five times the other, "Mixed" when it is not, "Insiders quiet" when there is neither. Filings do not say why anyone traded.
