How to read it: a report can list several trades made on different days, so the lag is shown as a range with its median. A report shows dollar ranges, not exact amounts, and it does not say why a trade was made; a late filing is a compliance fact, not proof of intent. Reports filed on paper are counted only when their trade dates could be read. Window: Oct. 30, 2026 to Nov. 24, 2026.
